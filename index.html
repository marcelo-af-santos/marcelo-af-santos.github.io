I’m a PhD student in theoretical physics at UC Davis and a member of the Center for Quantum Mathematics and Physics (QMAP), advised by Jaroslav Trnka. I study scattering amplitudes in quantum field theory and string theory, with a focus on the combinatorial and geometric structures underlying them. I’m also interested in pure spinor methods for maximally supersymmetric theories.
